# FitSmarter quick guide

Open <https://chimuel12.github.io/fitsmarter/> on your phone. Tap **?** in the top bar at any time to replay the in-app tour.

## 1. Install it

- **iPhone:** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** in Chrome, tap **Install** in the top bar, or use the ⋮ menu and choose **Install app**.

## 2. Set up your profile (You tab)

Under **Goals & profile**, fill in:

- daily goals for **Move** (kcal), **Exercise** (minutes) and **Distance** (km);
- **weight, height, sex and birth year**. Calories and strength medals depend on these.

## 3. Today

- **Rings:** fill all three every day. A brighter second lap shows when you go past a goal.
- **Rank card:** your place in your group this week, and how many points you need to catch the person ahead.
- **Streak**, workouts and active time this week.

## 4. Record a workout (+)

1. Pick a sport.
2. **Run, Walk, Ride or Hike** use GPS: a live map, distance, pace and calories. Keep the app open on screen while you move.
3. **Strength** opens a set logger. For each set, choose the exercise and enter the kg (per hand for dumbbells) and reps, then tap **Add set**. You see your medal straight away.
4. Other sports run a timer. You can also use **Log manually** for workouts you did earlier.
5. Tap **Finish**, give it a title if you like, and tap **Save**.

## 5. Strength medals

Each lift becomes an estimated one-rep max (Epley formula). That number is compared with strength standards for people of your **sex, body weight, age and height**. The result is your percentile:

| Medal | You are in the… |
|---|---|
| 💎 Diamond | top 1% |
| 🏆 Platinum | top 5% |
| 🥇 Gold | top 15% |
| 🥈 Silver | top 30% |
| 🥉 Bronze | top 50% |

The **You → Strength medals** card shows your best lift per exercise, where you sit on the medal bar, and exactly what you need for the next medal (for example "🥈 Silver at 105 kg 1RM (+12)").

Supported lifts: bench press, squat, deadlift, overhead press, barbell row, hip thrust, leg press, lat pulldown, dumbbell bench, dumbbell curl, pull-ups and push-ups.

*The standards are estimates based on public strength-standard tables. Use them for motivation, not as a test.*

## 6. Compete

- **New group:** give it a name, then tap **Invite friends** to send the link. Friends can also type the 6-letter code under **Join**.
- Leaderboards run **this week** (from Monday) or **this month**. You can rank by:
  - **Points:** 1 point per 10 kcal burned, so every sport counts;
  - **Minutes**, **Km** or **Workouts**;
  - **Medals:** the medals of your best lifts in that period (Bronze 1 … Diamond 5).
- The **Feed** shows everyone's workouts, routes and medals. Tap 👏 to give kudos.

## 7. Badges

There are 16 badges, for example: first workout, 10 and 50 workouts, 3- and 7-day streaks, 10 km, half marathon, early bird, night owl, 5 sports, snow day, 100 points in a week, and first, silver, gold and diamond medals.

## Privacy

- Your full activity history is visible only to you.
- Members of a group see the workouts you post while you're in it, plus your name and photo.
