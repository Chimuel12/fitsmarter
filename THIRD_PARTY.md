# Third-party data

## Exercise library: free-exercise-db

`data/exercises.json` and the exercise photos (loaded from jsDelivr) come from [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) by yuhonas. They are released into the public domain under the [Unlicense](https://unlicense.org/). The tier ratings and the Technogym machine entries are FitSmarter additions, built by `tools/build_library.py`.

## Body model: react-native-body-highlighter
